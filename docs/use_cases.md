# Use Cases

AI plus operations research is strongest where decisions are repetitive, constrained, high-value, and hard to coordinate manually.

## Workforce Rostering

Organizations can balance coverage, skills, fairness, fatigue, compliance, preferences, and cost. The value is not only better plans; it is faster replanning when demand changes.

## Manufacturing Dispatch

Factories can coordinate jobs, machines, materials, setup changes, delivery promises, and bottlenecks. AI agents help translate floor constraints into model artifacts, while optimization keeps the plan feasible.

## Logistics Optimization

Logistics teams can allocate loads, routes, hubs, time windows, vehicle capacity, and disruption response. A rule-first model makes service-level tradeoffs explicit instead of burying them in manual decisions.

## Inventory And Capacity Allocation

The framework direction applies to what to reserve, move, produce, or replenish when demand and capacity conflict. The model can show the cost of constraints and expose which assumptions drive the result.

## Cost Reduction And Efficiency

Cost reduction is often hidden in operational details: handoffs, idle capacity, overtime, empty movement, missed batching, and late decisions. AI OR systems can search those tradeoffs repeatedly while preserving human control over rules.

## A New Operating Pattern

The long-term opportunity is a new workflow for operations:

```text
Humans define policy.
AI agents structure the model.
Optimization engines search the decision space.
Diagnostics return evidence.
Teams revise the rules.
```

This loop can turn operational improvement from an occasional consulting project into a daily decision system.
